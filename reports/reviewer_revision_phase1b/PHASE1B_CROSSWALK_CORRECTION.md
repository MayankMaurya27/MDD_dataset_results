## 1. Forensic Audit of Phase 1B Crosswalk Gaps

A rigorous word-for-word audit of `reports/reviewer_revision_phase1b/PHASE1B_REVIEWER_REQUIREMENT_CROSSWALK.md` against the master project records reveals **six substantive structural deficiencies and omissions**:

1. **Omission of Fine Gaussian SVM Degeneracy Analysis**:
   - *Phase 1B Crosswalk Gap:* Grouped Fine Gaussian SVM under generic "8 classifiers" without acknowledging its catastrophic mathematical collapse (50.00% accuracy on EC, 52.46% on EO, 100% sensitivity, 0% specificity).
   - *Forensic Finding:* In 512-dimensional embedding space, setting fixed $\gamma = 10.0$ forces $K(\mathbf{x}_i, \mathbf{x}_j) = \exp(-10 \|\mathbf{x}_i - \mathbf{x}_j\|^2) \approx 0.0$ for all off-diagonal sample pairs. The kernel matrix degenerates into an identity matrix ($K \approx I$), reducing the SVM to a majority-class intercept predictor.
2. **Weakening of the "One-Participant Improvement" Statistical Scrutiny**:
   - *Phase 1B Crosswalk Gap:* Failed to isolate the reviewer's specific critique of incremental ablation gains.
   - *Forensic Finding:* In Exp 2 (channel weighting) and Exp 4 (nested tuning), accuracy improvements of $+1.72\%$ (from 89.66% to 91.38%) represent literally **one single participant** flipping from incorrect to correct ($52/58 \to 53/58$). Claiming structural neurophysiological superiority based on one subject without McNemar testing or permutation testing is an overstatement that reviewers specifically flagged.
3. **Omission of Abstract vs. Text Metric Discrepancies**:
   - *Phase 1B Crosswalk Gap:* Addressed manuscript text but omitted the legacy abstract discrepancies (where preliminary drafts cited 99.7% or confused segment vs. participant accuracy).
4. **Under-Specification of Complete-Pipeline Nested Model Selection**:
   - *Phase 1B Crosswalk Gap:* Framed nested CV primarily as SVM hyperparameter tuning ($C, \gamma$), omitting the representation/backbone choice (SPWVD vs. STFT vs. CWT; ResNet-18 vs. ResNet-50) from the inner validation loop.
5. **Separation of Standalone Repeated Group CV**:
   - *Phase 1B Crosswalk Gap:* Conflated repeated CV with nested CV rather than treating it as an independent requirement (10 independent random partition seeds $\times$ 10 folds = 100 total runs) to evaluate partition variance.
6. **Artifact-Controlled Pipeline Rerun Mandate**:
   - *Phase 1B Crosswalk Gap:* Treated artifact diagnostics as an observational report rather than establishing the experimental protocol for rerunning the proposed method and baselines on artifact-corrected signals.
